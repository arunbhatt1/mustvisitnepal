Who Are We?

A licensed trekking company founded and run by mountain guides in Thamel, Kathmandu.

Our Background

Must Visit Nepal started fifteen years ago as a small circle of independent guides from Solukhumbu and Rasuwa. After years of guiding for large overseas travel agencies, we chose to operate directly from Kathmandu so our clients could deal straight with the people leading them up the trail.

We keep our operation intentionally modest. We do not run fifty simultaneous departures. We manage small groups, assign one lead guide for every six trekkers, and ensure every porter carries no more than 15 kilograms.

When you book with us, you speak directly with our field guides, not a third-party sales agent halfway across the world.

Our Four Guiding Rules

Practical standards we follow on every single Himalayan departure.

1. Porter Protection

Porters are the backbone of Himalayan travel. We enforce a 15 kg weight ceiling per client, provide cold-weather outerwear and proper footwear, and insure every porter for high-altitude emergency medical rescue.

2. Acclimatization First

We do not shorten itineraries to offer cheaper prices. Every route above 3,500 meters includes scheduled acclimatization rest days and gradual elevation gains to prevent acute mountain sickness.

3. Direct Local Income

We lodge our groups exclusively in locally run family teahouses. The money you pay for dal bhat, tea, and room stays directly with the Sherpa, Tamang, and Gurung families living in the valleys.

4. Honest Advice

If trail weather is bad, if snow blocks a pass, or if a trekker shows symptoms of altitude sickness, we turn around or re-route. Safety and practical honesty always come before reaching a summit.
