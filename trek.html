Langtang Valley Trek

Glacial valley walk through Tamang villages and yak pastures north of Kathmandu

7 Days Moderate Teahouse trek Max: 4,773 m Distance: ~65 km Season: Mar–May, Sep–Nov

Trek Overview

A 7-day walk starting from Syabrubesi, following the Langtang River through rhododendron forest to the high valley of Kyanjin Gompa. You sleep in family-run teahouses and eat meals made by local lodge hosts.

Key Highlights

  • Kyanjin Gompa cheese dairy and active monastery
  • Panoramic mountain view from Tserko Ri viewpoint at 4,773 m
  • Rhododendron and bamboo forests in Langtang National Park
  • Traditional Tamang stone houses and carved mani walls

Day-by-Day Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Drive from Kathmandu to Syabrubesi (1,460 m)

    Morning bus or jeep drive north along the Trishuli River highway to Syabrubesi. Overnight at a teahouse.

  2. Day 2: Syabrubesi to Lama Hotel (2,470 m)

    Cross the Bhote Koshi suspension bridge and climb gradually through oak and rhododendron woods along the Langtang Khola.

  3. Day 3: Lama Hotel to Langtang Village (3,430 m)

    Walk upstream as the valley opens. Pass through Ghodatabela and reach the rebuilt stone houses of Langtang village.

  4. Day 4: Langtang Village to Kyanjin Gompa (3,870 m)

    Ascend past yak pastures and stone prayer walls to Kyanjin Gompa. Visit the local yak cheese production center in the afternoon.

  5. Day 5: Day hike to Tserko Ri (4,773 m) or Kyanjin Ri (4,350 m)

    Early morning ridge ascent for views of Langtang Lirung, Kinshung, and Yansa Tsenji. Return to Kyanjin Gompa for the night.

  6. Day 6: Kyanjin Gompa to Lama Hotel (2,470 m)

    Retrace the trail down the valley, descending back through the forest canopy to Lama Hotel.

  7. Day 7: Lama Hotel to Syabrubesi (1,460 m) and drive back to Kathmandu

    Final trail descent to Syabrubesi, followed by road transfer back to Kathmandu by afternoon.

What Is Included

  • Licensed English-speaking Nepali trekking guide
  • Porters to carry baggage (1 porter per 2 trekkers, max 15 kg per trekker)
  • Teahouse accommodation during the trek
  • Three meals daily (breakfast, lunch, dinner) from teahouse menus
  • Ground transportation between Kathmandu and Syabrubesi

What Is Excluded

  • Overseas flights and Nepal tourist entry visa
  • Personal travel and emergency helicopter evacuation insurance
  • Personal gear, sleeping bag, and hiking poles
  • Hot showers, Wi-Fi access, and battery charging at teahouses
  • Tips for guides and porters
