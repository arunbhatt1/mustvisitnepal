When to Trek in Nepal

Two distinct seasons offer the clearest skies and safest pass conditions.

Spring Season (March to May)

Rhododendrons and wild orchids bloom across middle hills. Days are warm at lower elevations, and high mountain passes gradually lose seasonal snow. Moderate daytime temperatures make for comfortable trail walking.

Autumn Season (September to November)

The monsoon rains wash the atmosphere clean, providing crystal-clear mountain vistas and steady daytime weather. This is the most popular time for high-altitude passes such as Thorong La and Kala Patthar.
