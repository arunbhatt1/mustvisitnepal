How We Run Our Treks

We are a small outfit based in Thamel, Kathmandu. We organize treks where you walk at a sensible pace and sleep in family-owned mountain lodges.

  • Porter care: Maximum load 15 kg per trekker, proper footwear, and rescue insurance.
  • Local food: Fresh dal bhat, seasonal greens, and Tibetan bread prepared daily in family kitchens.
  • Experienced leaders: Certified guides who grew up in the Solukhumbu, Langtang, and Annapurna regions.
